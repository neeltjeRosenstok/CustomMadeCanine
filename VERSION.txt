The Custom Made Canine v21.9.8 ONLINE TEST

Focused payment/accounting cleanup after v21.9.7 testing:
- Client payment screen is STK-first; manual M-Pesa is a fallback on the same screen rather than a separate booking-card payment method.
- Manual M-Pesa references must be exactly 10 alphanumeric characters; spaces are ignored and references are stored/displayed uppercase.
- Amy's Needs Attention wording now says M-Pesa Ref.
- Resume payment returns to the normal STK-first payment screen.
- Client credit is shown prominently at the top of Bookings and offered during checkout.
- Apply credit supports full or partial use. If credit exceeds the charge, the unused balance remains on the client account.
- Full-credit settlement still completes the booking/payment flow; partial credit continues to M-Pesa for the remaining balance.
- Payment receipt timestamps and M-Pesa receipt references are recorded for new automatic/manual payments.
- Payments report is accounting-first and sorted by payment received time: Received date/time, Client, Amount, M-Pesa Ref, Status, Refund/Credit, Source, Dog, Reference.
- Payments report date range is based on payment received/recorded time for new data; older records fall back to their existing created timestamp.
- Confirm & Pay is centered.
- Payment success now has one button: Back home, which returns the logged-in client to Client Portal > Bookings.
- Narrow-screen/iPhone date-input containment is applied globally, including the client private-booking date.

Still deliberately not included:
- Full class manual-payment recovery.
- Amy Payment received override (Cash payment received / Complimentary / Other).
- Client-tab attention badges and PWA notifications/icon work.
- eTIMS remains end-of-roadmap.
